# Treekin privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Treekin is a free, open-source family tree app (GPL-3.0). It is an independent app, not made or endorsed by Geni.com or MyHeritage.

## What stays on your device

- Your family trees (names, dates, places, notes, photos) are stored on your device in the app's private storage.
- Your Geni access token is stored encrypted on your device.

## What is sent to Geni

When you log in with your Geni account, Treekin talks to the Geni API (geni.com) on your behalf:

- it reads the profiles, relationships and photos you can see on Geni;
- it sends your edits (new, changed or deleted profiles, relationships and photos) to Geni.

Geni processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy: https://www.geni.com/home/privacy

## What we do not do

- No analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.
- No data is sent to the developer or to any server other than Geni.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Your choices

- Log out in the Geni account screen to remove the token from the device.
- Revoke Treekin's access at any time in your Geni account settings.
- Delete a tree in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove local data. Data already on Geni stays there until you delete it on Geni.
